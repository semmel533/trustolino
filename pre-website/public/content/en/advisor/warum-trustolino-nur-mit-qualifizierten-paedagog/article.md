## Quality instead of chance: The decisive difference

Many classic babysitter platforms operate on the principle of "open to everyone." This means: basically anyone can register, regardless of training or experience in the educational field. For parents, this often creates uncertainty. Who really is a good fit for my child? Who brings not just supervision, but also support?

Trustolino takes a different approach. Here, only people who have well-founded training or demonstrable experience in the educational field are placed – for example, educators, teaching students, or social pedagogues. This ensures that children are not just cared for, but also meaningfully guided.

A babysitter has long been more than just a supervisor. Children need interaction, support, and individual care, especially in important developmental phases. Exactly here lies the strength of an educational babysitter platform like Trustolino.

Educationally qualified caregivers bring a deep understanding of child development. They know:

Specifically, this means: While your child is being cared for, they simultaneously experience valuable time in which creativity, language, or social skills are strengthened.
