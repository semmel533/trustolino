## Babysitter Hourly Wage Mannheim: Realistic Ranges

The pay depends heavily on qualifications, experience, and tasks. In Mannheim, the following ranges have become established:

- Pupils: 8 to 12 € per hour

- Babysitters without pedagogical training: 10 to 14 € per hour

- Pedagogy students: 14 to 18 € per hour

- Trained professionals: 18 to 25 € per hour As a qualified professional, you should not work for less than 18 €. Anything below that usually means that you are not communicating your value correctly. By the way: With a registered employment (e.g., mini-job), the statutory minimum wage of 13.90 € per hour (as of 2026) applies as an absolute lower limit for adults anyway.

## Why Pedagogues Earn More

Parents today are looking for more than supervision; they are looking for trust, safety, and development for their child. As a pedagogue, you bring competencies that classic babysitters cannot offer:

- Structured care with a concept
